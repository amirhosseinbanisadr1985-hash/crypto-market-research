# Crypto Market Research & Tokenomics Analysis

Independent research and analytical case studies covering cryptocurrency markets, token economics, blockchain ecosystems, and Web3 projects.

**Author:** Amir Hossein Banisadr  
**Location:** Barcelona, Spain

## Research Focus

- Tokenomics: supply, distribution, incentives, and token utility
- Fundamental analysis of cryptocurrency projects
- Competitive positioning and ecosystem research
- Business models and value capture mechanisms
- Market risks and adoption challenges
- Research-based investment evaluation (not financial advice)

## Featured Research: Chainlink (LINK)

An independent research case study examining Chainlink's ecosystem, LINK token utility, tokenomics, competitive positioning, value capture mechanisms, and key risks.

The research report will be uploaded to this repository.

## Research Approach

1. Define the research question and project scope.
2. Collect and cross-check information from primary and reputable sources.
3. Evaluate token utility, supply dynamics, adoption, and economic incentives.
4. Compare the project with relevant competitors.
5. Identify assumptions, limitations, and key risks.
6. Present findings in a clear and structured report.
